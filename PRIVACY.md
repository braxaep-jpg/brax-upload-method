# Privacy Policy for Brax Upload Method

**Effective Date:** January 2026

## Overview

Brax Upload Method ("we", "us", "our", or "Company") operates the brax-upload-method.com website and the Brax Upload Method browser extension (collectively, the "Service").

This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our Service and the choices you have associated with that data.

## Information Collection and Use

### A. Information We Collect

1. **Video Files**
   - You voluntarily upload video files for analysis and optimization
   - Videos are processed temporarily on our servers
   - Videos are deleted after processing (not stored permanently)
   - Video files are never shared with third parties

2. **Technical Information**
   - Device type and operating system
   - Browser type and version
   - IP address (standard web logging)
   - Usage patterns and anonymized analytics

3. **Video Metadata** (Extracted from your files)
   - Resolution, frame rate, bitrate
   - Codec information
   - Duration and color space
   - **No personal content is extracted** - only technical parameters

### B. How We Use Information

- To provide and improve the Service
- To generate recommendations for video optimization
- To fix technical issues and improve performance
- To understand usage patterns (anonymized)
- To comply with legal obligations

## Data Storage and Security

- Video files are stored temporarily in `/tmp` directory
- Files are automatically deleted after processing (within 1 hour)
- We use HTTPS encryption for all data transmission
- Backend servers do not retain video files permanently
- No personal information is sold to third parties

## Browser Extension Permissions

The Brax Upload Method extension requests the following permissions:

| Permission | Purpose | Data Collected |
|-----------|---------|-----------------|
| `storage` | Store user preferences locally | User settings (local only) |
| `*://*.tiktok.com/*` | Monitor TikTok upload pages | None - only detects file selection events |

**Important:** The extension:
- Does NOT access TikTok account information
- Does NOT track your activity outside TikTok
- Does NOT intercept passwords or personal data
- ALL data processing happens locally in your browser

## Third-Party Services

We may use the following services:

### Analytics
- Google Analytics (anonymized, IP anonymization enabled)
- No personally identifiable information is sent

### Hosting
- Vercel (Frontend)
- Railway/Heroku (Backend)

These providers have their own privacy policies.

### Video Processing
- FFmpeg (open-source, local processing)
- No data is sent to external processing services
